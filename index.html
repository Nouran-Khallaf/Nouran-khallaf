About

About Me

I am a PhD-qualified NLP researcher with over 14 years of experience in computational linguistics, text simplification, readability analysis and applied language technology. My central question is how complex public-facing information can be made easier to understand and use without losing essential meaning.

Across the Horizon Europe iDEM project, UCREL and my doctoral research, I develop multilingual corpora, annotation frameworks, simplification pipelines, web-based research software and APIs, and explainable, uncertainty-aware models for accessible and reliable communication.

Research

Selected Research Contributions

My research generates corpora, annotation frameworks, software and modelling methods that support more accessible public communication across languages and audiences.

01 · Accessibility

Readability and Text Simplification

Sentence-level readability modelling, Easy-to-Read complexity annotation and simplification pipelines for democratic and institutional information within the iDEM project.

02 · Arabic NLP

Arabic Simplification Resources

A corpus-based Modern Standard Arabic simplification system, Arabic readability resources and CEFR-oriented lexical support for an under-resourced language.

03 · Reliability

Explainable and Uncertainty-Aware NLP

Interpretability, noise robustness and uncertainty estimation for deciding when readability models are reliable and when predictions need caution.

04 · Tools

Open Software and Language Resources

Research software and reusable data for bilingual survey analysis, Welsh thesaurus development, multilingual corpora and public-facing language technology.
